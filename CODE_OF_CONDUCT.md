# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PHPAML contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing private communication.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents PHPAML. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation. Retaliation is prohibited.

## Français

La communauté PHPAML s’engage à offrir un environnement accueillant, respectueux et sans harcèlement. Les désaccords doivent porter sur les idées et le code, jamais sur les personnes. Signalez confidentiellement tout problème par le moyen indiqué dans [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Les représailles sont interdites.
